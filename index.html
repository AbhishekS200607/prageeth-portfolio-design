Identity & Philosophy

BEHIND THE VISUALS.

PRAGEETH P. VISUAL ARTIST

I'm Prageeth P., an independent graphic designer passionate about creating meaningful visual experiences, strong identities and expressive design compositions.

Grounded in the purity of Swiss typography, brutalist geometry, and minimalist editorial discipline, my work explores the delicate balance between structural tension and artistic restraint. Every composition is an intentional dialogue between negative space, bold typography, and visual storytelling.

Whether designing an avant-garde poster series, architecting an entire corporate visual identity, or engineering tactile luxury packaging, I believe exceptional graphic design does not shout — it commands attention through uncompromising conviction.
